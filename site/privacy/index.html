Privacy, cookies and accessibility

This website is designed to collect as little information about you as possible.

Privacy

This website is run by Mr Krishna Boddu, who is the data controller for it. The website has no forms and does not ask for your personal or medical details.

  • Hosting: our hosting provider, Netlify, records technical information such as IP addresses in server logs to keep the site secure. We do not use this information to identify you.
  • Visitor statistics: if we measure visits, we use a privacy-friendly service that does not use cookies or identify individual visitors.
  • Contacting us: if you call or email Daniela, we use your details only to arrange and provide your care.

You have the right to ask what information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Please contact Daniela on 01473 279148 or at daniela.capaldo@nuffieldhealth.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies. Links to other websites, such as Nuffield Health's booking page, Doctify and Top Doctors, are covered by those websites' own privacy and cookie policies.

Accessibility

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. You should be able to zoom to 200% without losing content, navigate using a keyboard or screen reader, and read all text clearly. If you have difficulty using any part of the site, or need information in another format, please contact Daniela.

Medical information

The information on this website is general and does not replace a consultation. It is written and reviewed by Mr Krishna Boddu.
