About Mr Krishna Boddu

I am a consultant orthopaedic surgeon specialising in hip and knee replacement, including complex and revision surgery. I work at Ipswich Hospital in the NHS and see private patients at Nuffield Health Ipswich Hospital.

My approach

My practice is evidence-based. I recommend surgery only when the likely benefits clearly outweigh the risks for you, and I explain both in plain language. Many people with hip and knee problems do well without an operation, and I will tell you if that is the case for you. If you do have surgery with me, I will look after you from your first appointment through to your follow-up after the operation.

Training and experience

  • Postgraduate orthopaedic training at PGIMER, Chandigarh, India
  • Research in orthopaedic biomechanics at Imperial College London
  • Registrar training in trauma and orthopaedic surgery in South East London
  • Fellowships in primary and revision hip and knee replacement at Maisonneuve-Rosemont Hospital, Montreal, and King's College Hospital, London
  • NHS consultant orthopaedic surgeon since 2015
  • Consultant at King's College Hospital NHS Foundation Trust, London, before moving to Ipswich Hospital (East Suffolk and North Essex NHS Foundation Trust) in 2022
  • More than 1,000 hip and knee replacements, with my operations recorded on the National Joint Registry
  • Trained in Mako robotic-assisted hip and knee replacement

Qualifications
