Second opinions on hip and knee surgery

If you have been advised to have hip or knee surgery, or an operation has not gone as you hoped, a second opinion can help you decide with confidence. Asking for one is common and sensible before major surgery.

What I offer

  • A review of your history, letters and scans
  • A full examination
  • A clear explanation of all your options, including not having surgery
  • A written summary for you and, with your consent, your GP

What to bring

  • Letters from other specialists, and any operation notes
  • Your scan reports, and the images if you can get them
  • A list of your medicines

Daniela can help arrange for images to be sent to the hospital before your appointment.

Insurance

Most insurers cover a second opinion with a GP referral. Please check with your insurer before booking. See fees and insurance.
