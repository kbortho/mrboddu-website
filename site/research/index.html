Research and publications

Alongside my clinical work, I have carried out and published research on hip and knee surgery. Here is a selection, with a short plain-English summary of each.

I have published 25 research papers, which have been cited more than 700 times by other researchers. My research has mainly been on hip and knee surgery: how implants perform over the long term, how to plan and position them accurately, and how to reduce complications. More recently, I have worked on using artificial intelligence to read X-rays.

Research matters for patients. It is the reason I base my advice on the best available evidence, and choose implants with long, proven track records.

Some of my earlier papers were published under the names Rama KR or Boddu Siva Rama KR.

Hip replacement and hip surgery

  1. Boddu K. Three-year and five-year revision rates of primary total hip arthroplasty are poor predictors of 15-year revision rates: a National Joint Registry analysis. Orthopaedic Proceedings 2023;105-B(Supp 11):39 (British Hip Society).

    Using National Joint Registry data, I found that how well a hip implant performs in its first 3 to 5 years is a poor guide to how it will perform at 15 years. This is one reason I use implants with a long, proven track record.

  2. Somerville CMB, Geddes JA, Tofighi M, Boddu K. Accuracy and reproducibility of visual estimation of the acetabular cup positioning in total hip arthroplasty on plain radiographs by orthopaedic surgeons. Journal of Perioperative Practice 2021.

    Surgeons were not always accurate at judging the angle of a hip socket by eye on an X-ray, so we recommended measuring it with specialist software.

  3. Boddu K, Siebachmeyer M, Lakkol S, Rajayogeswaran B, Kavarthapu V, Li PL. Predicting the underestimation of the femoral offset in anteroposterior radiographs of the pelvis using the ‘lesser trochanter index’: a 3D CT derived simulated radiographic analysis. Journal of Arthroplasty 2014.

    A new way of telling when a standard X-ray underestimates the hip’s natural offset, which helps in planning a hip replacement that restores the hip’s mechanics.

  4. Siebachmeyer M, Lakkol S, Boddu K, et al. Can reduced hip offset increase wear in hard-on-hard hip bearings? Bone & Joint Journal (Orthopaedic Proceedings) 2014;96-B(Supp 18):16.

    How the position of a hip replacement may affect wear of metal and ceramic bearings.

  5. Lakkol S, Boddu K, Buckle C, Kavarthapu V, Li P. Significant fixation failure in internal fixation of undisplaced intracapsular hip fracture. Bone & Joint Journal (Orthopaedic Proceedings) 2014;96-B(Supp 18):17.

    How often fixing an undisplaced hip fracture with screws fails.

  6. Rama KR, Vendittoli PA, Ganapathi M, Borgman R, Roy A, Lavigne M. Heterotopic ossification after surface replacement arthroplasty and total hip arthroplasty: a randomized study. Journal of Arthroplasty 2009.

    A randomised trial comparing extra bone formation around the hip after hip resurfacing and after total hip replacement.

  7. Lavigne M, Rama KR, Ganapathi M, Nuño N, Vendittoli PA. Factors affecting acetabular bone loss during primary hip arthroplasty: a quantitative analysis using computer simulation. Clinical Biomechanics 2008.

    A computer study of how much bone is removed from the socket during hip replacement, and the factors that affect it.

Knee replacement

  1. Chrastek D, Boddu K. Long-term survival rates are similar with or without patellar resurfacing after total knee arthroplasty; however, higher revision rate without patellar resurfacing can be prosthesis-specific: a National Joint Registry analysis. Orthopaedic Proceedings 2023;105-B(Supp 13):85 (British Association for Surgery of the Knee).

    At 15 years, knee replacements did equally well whether or not the back of the kneecap was resurfaced, although for some implant designs leaving it unresurfaced led to more revisions.

  2. Rama KR, Apsingi S, Poovali S, Jetti A. Timing of tourniquet release in knee arthroplasty: meta-analysis of randomized, controlled trials. Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery (American) 2007;89:699–705.

    A review of 11 randomised trials. Releasing the tourniquet early increased blood loss, while releasing it only after the wound was closed may increase the risk of early complications.

  3. Reddi BSK, Rai J, Suri S. Study of factors influencing range of motion after total knee arthroplasty. Indian Journal of Orthopaedics 2002.

    The factors that affect how far the knee bends after a knee replacement.

Knee biomechanics

Laboratory research carried out during my research fellowship at Imperial College London.

  1. Cuomo P, Rama KR, Bull AMJ, Amis AA. The effects of different tensioning strategies on knee laxity and graft tension after double-bundle anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction. American Journal of Sports Medicine 2007.

    How the way a reconstructed ligament is tensioned affects the stability of the knee.

  2. Khan R, Konyves A, Rama KR, Thomas R, Amis AA. RSA can measure ACL graft stretching and migration: development of a new method. Clinical Orthopaedics and Related Research 2006.

    A new X-ray technique for measuring whether a reconstructed knee ligament stretches or slips after surgery.

  3. Amis AA, Cuomo P, Boddu Siva Rama KR, et al. Measurement of knee laxity and pivot-shift kinematics with magnetic sensors. Operative Techniques in Orthopaedics 2008.

    Using magnetic sensors to measure knee movement and stability.

  4. Cuomo P, Boddu Siva Rama KR, Bull AMJ, Amis AA. On the relative contribution of the two main anterior cruciate ligament functional bundles to intact knee kinematics. Arthroscopy 2012;28(8):e218–9 (ISAKOS).

    How the two parts of the anterior cruciate ligament each contribute to normal knee movement.

  5. Boddu Siva Rama KR, Cuomo P, Bull A, Thomas R, Amis AA. Validation of a novel non-invasive tracking system for measuring three-dimensional knee laxity in vivo. Journal of Biomechanics 2006;39(Suppl 1):S72–3.

    Testing a new, non-invasive way of measuring how loose or stable a knee is.

Artificial intelligence and research methods

  1. Boddu K, Siebachmeyer M, Lakkol S, Jain J. Relook MSK, an AI tool that analyses musculoskeletal radiographs, can potentially save £200 million annually for the NHS. Poster, AI in Orthopaedics, Royal College of Surgeons of England, London, September 2022.

    An artificial intelligence tool that reads bone and joint X-rays, and its potential savings for the NHS.

  2. Rama KR, Poovali S, Apsingi S. Quality of reporting of orthopaedic diagnostic accuracy studies is suboptimal. Clinical Orthopaedics and Related Research 2006.

    Many orthopaedic studies of diagnostic tests did not report their methods well enough to judge how reliable they were.
