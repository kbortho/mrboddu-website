I am a consultant orthopaedic surgeon at Ipswich Hospital, and I see private patients at Nuffield Health Ipswich Hospital. I look after people with hip and knee problems from their first assessment through to surgery and recovery. My practice is evidence-based: many people do not need an operation, and I will tell you if that is the case for you.
