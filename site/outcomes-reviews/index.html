Outcomes and patient reviews

You can check my results and what patients say through independent organisations that collect and publish them.

National Joint Registry

Every hip and knee replacement I perform is recorded on the National Joint Registry, which publishes data for each surgeon, including whether their results are within the expected range. See my National Joint Registry profile.

My hip replacement results

This chart compares how often hip replacements need to be redone (a "revision") for every surgeon in the registry:

  • Each light blue dot is another surgeon.
  • The green line at 1 is the expected number of revisions, after allowing for patients' age and health. A dot above the line means more revisions than expected, and below it means fewer.
  • My black dot is at about 0.3, which means my patients have needed fewer than a third of the revisions expected.

In other words, the survival of my hip replacements is better than expected in the National Joint Registry.

Private Healthcare Information Network (PHIN)

PHIN publishes information about consultants in private practice. On PHIN, 96% of 76 patients surveyed said they were extremely or very likely to recommend me (profile updated October 2026). See my PHIN profile.
