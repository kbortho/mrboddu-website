Fees and insurance

My fees are below. If you are paying for yourself, Nuffield Health Ipswich gives you a fixed-price package for surgery after your consultation, so you know the cost before you decide.

Insurance

I am recognised by:

  • Bupa
  • AXA Health
  • Aviva
  • Vitality
  • WPA
  • Allianz
  • Healix

I am a Bupa fee-assured consultant, so Bupa members pay no shortfall on my fees for eligible treatment (your policy excess may still apply). If your insurer is not listed, please ask Daniela.

  1. Check that your policy covers outpatient consultations and the treatment you may need.
  2. Ask your GP for a referral letter if your insurer needs one.
  3. Call your insurer for a pre-authorisation number, and give it to Daniela when you book.

Paying for yourself

Nuffield Health Ipswich offers fixed-price packages for surgery, which usually include the hospital stay, the surgeon's and anaesthetist's fees and follow-up care; your quote will set out exactly what is included. Finance options are also available through Nuffield Health. See Nuffield Health Ipswich Hospital.
