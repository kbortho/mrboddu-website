Hip and knee surgery for patients from Colchester and north Essex

I see many patients from Colchester, Manningtree, Harwich, Clacton, Frinton and the Dedham Vale. My private clinic is at Nuffield Health Ipswich Hospital, a short drive up the A12, and I carry out most revision surgery at Colchester Hospital.

Where you will be seen

  • Consultations: at Nuffield Health Ipswich Hospital, Nuffield Health Ipswich Hospital, Foxhall Road, Ipswich, IP4 5SW. My clinic runs on Monday, 8.30am to 12.30pm. Get directions
  • Hip and knee replacement: at Nuffield Health Ipswich Hospital.

Where I carry out revision surgery

Revision surgery is a bigger operation, and patients may need high-dependency care afterwards. For this reason, I prefer to carry out most revision operations at the Essex and Suffolk Elective Orthopaedic Centre (ESEOC) at Colchester Hospital, which offers a private patient service for both insured and self-paying patients. Some less complicated revision operations are done at Nuffield Health Ipswich. We will discuss which hospital is best for you at your first consultation at Nuffield Health Ipswich.

What I treat

  • Hip replacement and knee replacement, including robotic-assisted surgery where appropriate
  • Partial knee replacement
  • Revision hip and revision knee replacement
  • Knee arthroscopy and meniscal surgery
  • Second opinions
